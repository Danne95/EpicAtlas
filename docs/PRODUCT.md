# Product brief

A local travel atlas that makes personal history the focus of a dark 2D world map and switchable 3D globe.

- Render saved city boundaries with glowing outlines; preserve legacy points without drawing dots.
- Show places, road, rail, water and flight journeys with independent visibility controls.
- Support local editing, normalized route imports, backups, date filtering and cumulative playback.
- Keep personal history separate from replaceable global geography. Start empty, with no demo fallback.
- Load installed code, world geography and saved travel locally. Network acquisition requires explicit lookup, preview or installation actions.
- Favor clear visualization over navigation-grade GIS: flight heights are stylized, road/rail suggestions require review, and water connections are approximate.
- Disable detailed regional basemaps. Saving/viewing a city never installs street or building assets.

Offline regional preparation and conservative cleanup remain separate tools for existing local assets. Optional extensions are in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md).
