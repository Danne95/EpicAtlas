# EpicAtlas

**Your travels. One beautiful world.**

Turn the places you've been and the journeys you've taken into a personal atlas. Watch cities glow against a dark world map, follow your routes across continents, and switch to a 3D globe to see it all from a new perspective.

From a weekend away to years of exploring, EpicAtlas brings your travel story together.

## See where life has taken you

- **Light up your cities.** Mark the places you've visited with glowing city outlines.
- **Connect your journeys.** Add flights, road trips, train rides and water journeys, each with its own color.
- **Explore your world.** Move between a flat map and a 3D globe, zoom into places, and choose which journeys to show.
- **Watch your story unfold.** Look back at a particular year or play your dated travels in order as your map fills in.
- **Make it yours.** Add names and dates, update your entries, and bring in recorded routes.

Your atlas starts empty, ready for your own memories.

## Open your atlas

Once EpicAtlas is set up on Windows, double-click the **EpicAtlas** desktop shortcut. It opens in your browser, ready to explore. You can also double-click **Start EpicAtlas.cmd** in the project folder.

Keep the **EpicAtlas - close to stop** window open while you use it. Close that window when you're finished; closing the browser alone leaves the app running.

First time here? Follow the [setup guide](docs/SETUP.md). The shortcut uses an existing installation; it doesn't install anything for you.

## Add a memory in a few steps

1. Open **Edit travel** and choose a place or journey type.
2. Find a city boundary, enter your flight's airport codes, or choose the stops for your journey.
3. Add an optional date, review any suggested route, and choose **Save record**.
4. Close the editor to see your updated map.

Only remember the month or year? That's enough. Dates are optional, and you can fill in the details later.

Road and rail suggestions are yours to review before saving. Water connections are approximate and can cross land; flight curves illustrate the connection between airports. EpicAtlas helps you remember your travels rather than navigate your next trip.

## Your memories stay on your computer

Your travel history saves on this device. After setup, you can explore saved journeys and installed maps without an internet connection.

Finding a new place or requesting a road or rail suggestion needs internet. Those actions share the place names or route coordinates needed for the search; your saved history, journey names and dates stay local. Maps and the airport list are downloaded separately during setup.

Use **Export backup** in the editor to keep an extra copy or move your atlas to another computer. Restoring a backup replaces your current atlas, so keep a copy before switching. Local files aren't encrypted; protect them as you would other personal files.
